technical philosophy

How I think about the stack

I believe good technology starts with good policy. Company policy gets enforced through the technology itself, and documented clearly so results stay reliable and repeatable. That same care carries through every layer: the network, the services on it, the operating system, the applications, the files, and the hardware underneath.
